The Cape Cod Fall Deck Window Closes Soon. Stonewood Has the Boards on the Floor.

By American Pro September 9, 2026 5 min read

Labor Day has passed and the Cape's building season is on the clock. If you want a new deck finished, inspected, and usable before the holidays, this is the window to order the boards. Stonewood Products has American Pro Legacy PVC decking in Driftwood and Khaki in stock right now at both its Harwich and Mashpee locations, which means the material is not the thing standing between you and a finished project this year. Time is.

Why September is the real deadline

Most homeowners think about deck timing backward. They picture the install date and work forward from there, when the schedule actually has to be built backward from the weather. On Cape Cod, a PVC deck can go down comfortably into November, but the steps before the install, permitting, contractor scheduling, and footing work in ground that has not yet frozen, all compress fast once fall weather arrives. Builders who take fall work are already booking into their remaining open weeks. The homeowners who call this month get on that calendar. The homeowners who call in late October are looking at spring.

Buying the boards early does not commit you to a date. It just means that once a contractor has an opening, or once you decide to tackle the job yourself, the material is not the bottleneck. Stonewood has both colors on the floor today, in the lengths most Cape decks need, ready for same-visit pickup.

Two colors, already stocked

Stonewood carries American Pro Legacy PVC decking in Driftwood and Khaki, the two tones that read most naturally against the Cape's grey-shingle and weathered-cedar homes. Both are solid, through-the-board cellular PVC with a low-gloss wood-grain surface, so there is no film to wear through and no color layer to fade unevenly. Neither will rot, splinter, or wick moisture the way a wood deck does after one more Cape winter.

A simple order-to-done timeline

  1. Now through mid-September
    Confirm color, measure the deck, and pick up or order boards at Stonewood. This is the step most people delay and the one with the most schedule flexibility right now.
  2. Late September
    Lock a contractor date or block your own install weekend. Ground is still workable and daylight is still long enough for a full day on the deck.
  3. October
    Install window for most Cape jobs. Cellular PVC installs the same in cool fall weather as it does in July, no special handling required.
  4. By the holidays
    Deck finished, railing set, and ready for use well before anyone is hosting a holiday gathering on it.

None of these dates are hard deadlines. They are the difference between a deck you use this year and one you plan for next spring. The only step in that timeline that depends on us is the first one, and the boards are already sitting on the floor in Harwich and Mashpee.
