Which One Is You?

ChooseLegacy PVC if

  • You want a clean, classic solid color
  • The job is commercial
  • You have face-fastened spots, like stair treads and the board against the house
  • You want the line dealers stock first

ChooseTrueGrain if

  • You want the deepest hardwood-grain look
  • You want a cool-underfoot surface in full sun
  • The project is residential
  • You want a premium hardwood appearance
Before you order

Joists can run at most 16 in. on center on both lines, and Legacy PVC calls for 12 in. on center for heavy loads or angled installs. Confirm board size and stock lengths for your line with a dealer or with us before you finalize a layout. Both lines ship factory-direct from Linden, and Legacy PVC is also stocked at Stonewood Products on Cape Cod.
