What is TrueGrain Deck?

TrueGrain Deck is American Pro's flagship capped cellular PVC decking. A decorative wood-grain foil is laminated to a weatherable ASA capstock, which seals a moisture-resistant cellular PVC core on all four sides. The result is a board that looks and feels like premium hardwood with the strength and low-maintenance profile of cellular PVC.

What colors does TrueGrain Deck come in?

Six hardwood-inspired colors: New England Birch (weathered silver-grey), Coastal Driftwood (soft warm grey), Aged Oak (golden tan, open grain), Embered Taupe (warm smoky brown), Royal IPE (deep mahogany amber), and Tropical Walnut (dark walnut grain).

What is the TrueGrain warranty?

The TrueGrain decorative exterior foil carries a 25-year limited residential warranty against abnormal color change beyond 2 Delta E and against cracking. Full coverage years 1 through 5, prorated thereafter. Residential installations only.

Can TrueGrain be installed with hidden fasteners?

Yes. Grooved TrueGrain profiles install with the InvisiClip hidden fastener system, powered by the Grad System. Engineered POM clips snap onto pre-fastened aluminum rails so boards drop in from above with no visible screws.

Does TrueGrain work with universal side-clip systems too?

Is TrueGrain cool to walk on barefoot?

Yes. TrueGrain Deck uses cool-deck technology that reflects more solar energy than dark composite decking. The capstock and decorative foil are formulated to stay comfortable underfoot through the warmest part of the afternoon, especially in lighter colors.

Is TrueGrain Deck made in the USA?

How do I maintain TrueGrain decking?

Routine cleaning with soap and water is all that is needed. Because TrueGrain is capped cellular PVC with no exposed wood fiber, it never needs sanding, staining, or sealing the way wood does, and it will not splinter, rot, or grey out over time.

InvisiClip™

InvisiClip™,
answered.

The hidden fastener system, powered by the Grad System, that drops grooved boards in from above with no visible screws.

What is InvisiClip™?

InvisiClip™ is American Pro's hidden fastener system for grooved PVC decking, powered by the Grad System. Engineered POM polymer clips snap onto aluminum rails. The rails sit on top of each joist running parallel to the joists at 16 inches on center maximum. Boards drop in from above so no screws or fasteners show on the finished deck.

What spec does InvisiClip™ follow?

InvisiClip™ follows the Grad System component spec. The clips, rails, and accessories are dimensionally compatible with the published Grad install method. On American Pro decking, joists must be 16 inches on center maximum and a rail sits on top of every joist, so rails are also 16 inches on center maximum. Start Rail and Mini Rail fastener layouts and removability with Grad keys are unchanged.

Are the deck boards removable after install?

Yes. Boards installed on InvisiClip™ Start Rails can be lifted out individually using Grad keys, which release the snap engagement so a single board can be replaced without disturbing the rest of the deck. Mini Rails are a fixed alternative used at board ends.

What screws and nails does InvisiClip™ use?

Type 316 stainless steel screws (#8 or #10, 1.5 inch length, Simpson Strong Tie T08162WPB) for screw fastening, or 0.148 inch by 1.5 inch hot-dip galvanized class D nails (N10D5HDG-R) for nail-gun installation.

Has InvisiClip™ been independently tested for wind uplift?

Yes. American Pro grooved decking installed with InvisiClip™ technology was tested by Intertek Building & Construction (TL-144 IAS-accredited, ISO/IEC 17025) under ICC-ES AC174 Section 4.1.4 using ASTM E330 methods. Three test specimens reached an average ultimate uplift of 306 psf and an average sustained uplift of 292 psf at a 10 second hold before clip release. The full Intertek report R1545.01-119-19 R0 dated 08/05/24 is available for download from the InvisiClip™ page.

Which American Pro decking works with InvisiClip™?

Both decking lines. The same groove geometry is extruded on grooved TrueGrain Deck and grooved Legacy PVC Decking, so InvisiClip™ works with either line using the same clips and rails.

What is the maximum joist spacing for InvisiClip™?

Joists must be 16 inches on center maximum, and a rail sits on top of every joist, so rails run at 16 inches on center maximum as well. This follows the Grad System install method.

Where can I get InvisiClip™ components?

InvisiClip™ clips, rails, and fasteners are supplied alongside American Pro grooved decking. Reach out through the contact form on this page for component details and availability.

Legacy PVC Decking

Legacy PVC Decking,
answered.

The original solid-color cellular PVC deck board, made on the same line as TrueGrain in a classic six-color palette.

What is Legacy PVC Decking?

Legacy PVC Decking is American Pro's original cellular PVC deck board. The board is solid-color UV-stabilized PVC with a subtle wood-grain texture, manufactured on the same extrusion line as our flagship TrueGrain Deck. The difference is the surface finish: Legacy PVC carries a classic six-color palette, TrueGrain carries a laminated hardwood-grain surface.

What colors does Legacy PVC Decking come in?

Six classic colors: Driftwood (light grey wood-grain), Khaki (warm tan), Hazelnut (medium warm brown), Beachwood (cool grey grain), Chestnut (rich red-brown), and Redwood (warm reddish-brown).

What profiles are available?

Two profiles. Solid Edge boards are traditional square-edge boards that are top-fastened with screws and color-matched plugs. Grooved boards install with our InvisiClip hidden fastener system, leaving no visible screws on the finished deck surface.

Can Legacy PVC Decking be installed with hidden fasteners?

Yes. Grooved Legacy PVC boards are dimensionally compatible with the InvisiClip system, which is built to the Grad System spec. The same groove geometry is extruded on both Legacy PVC and TrueGrain Deck, so InvisiClip works with either line. Because that groove is a standard geometry, grooved Legacy boards also accept universal side-edge hidden clip systems made for grooved decking, such as CAMO EDGE and WEDGE clips and Starborn Clip & Rip, so installers can use the hidden fastener line they already stock.

Can Legacy PVC Decking be face-fastened with the Starborn Pro Plug system?

Yes. The Starborn Pro Plug System is a matched face-fastening setup for Legacy PVC Decking, ideal for the spots that have to be top-fastened anyway, such as stair treads, the perimeter board against the house, and the last course at a wall. A stepped bit pre-drills and counterbores in one pass, then stainless screws are driven and tapered cellular PVC plugs are tapped in and sanded flush. Because Legacy is solid color all the way through, the color-matched plug blends into the board and ages with the deck instead of standing out. The Pro Plug system is matched to Legacy specifically and is not used on TrueGrain Deck, whose laminated wood-grain surface is meant to stay uninterrupted.

What is the Legacy PVC warranty?

Legacy PVC Decking carries a 25-year limited residential warranty and a 10-year limited commercial warranty. Boards are warranted against splitting, flaking, rotting, and structural manufacturing defects. The board is also designed to resist fading and will not fade more than 5 Delta E (CIE) units after exposure to UV and weathering.

How is Legacy PVC different from TrueGrain Deck?

Both boards share the same cellular PVC core and extrusion line. Legacy PVC is solid-color UV-stabilized PVC with a subtle wood-grain texture in a classic six-color palette. TrueGrain adds a laminated hardwood-grain capstock finish for a more realistic wood look. Choose Legacy for a clean classic finish and TrueGrain for a premium hardwood appearance.

Is Legacy PVC Decking made in the USA?

Yes. Legacy PVC Decking is extruded by Patwin Plastics at its vertically integrated facility in Linden, New Jersey, on the same line as TrueGrain Deck.

Can I order free Legacy PVC samples?

Yes. Free hand samples of any Legacy PVC color can be requested through the samples form on this page so you can evaluate the color and grain on the actual home before ordering.

What is the best porch flooring to buy?

The best porch flooring to buy is cellular PVC tongue-and-groove flooring, because it won't rot, splinter, cup, or grey out the way wood does and won't sag or stain like wood-plastic composite. Compared against pressure-treated wood, natural hardwood like IPE, tile, stone, and concrete, cellular PVC is the only option that stays low-maintenance and slip-resistant for decades. American Pro PVC porch flooring is engineered cellular PVC with a wood-grain finish, available in 3/4 inch and 7/8 inch thicknesses and six colors, slip, fire and fade resistant, made in the USA, and backed by a 25-year stain and fade limited warranty.

What porch flooring options should I compare before buying?

Most homeowners compare six porch flooring materials: cellular PVC, wood-plastic composite, pressure-treated wood, natural hardwood such as IPE, tile or stone, and poured concrete. Weigh each one on rot and moisture resistance, splintering, fade resistance, slip resistance, maintenance, and lifetime cost. Cellular PVC scores best on durability and upkeep, which is why it is the best porch flooring to buy for a porch you don't want to replace again.

Is PVC porch flooring better than composite or wood?

Yes. Cellular PVC has no wood fiber, so unlike composite it can't absorb moisture, swell, or stain, and unlike pressure-treated wood it never needs sanding, staining, or sealing. PVC won't splinter or grey out and stays slip-resistant in rain and snow, making it the longest-lasting, lowest-maintenance porch flooring to buy.

What makes American Pro the best PVC porch flooring?

American Pro PVC porch flooring is solid cellular PVC with an embossed wood-grain finish, so it stays slip, fire and fade resistant and never rots, splinters, cups, or greys out. The tongue-and-groove PVC porch flooring boards come in 3/4 inch and 7/8 inch thicknesses and six colors, install the same way as wood porch flooring with standard tools, are made in the USA in Linden, NJ, and carry a 25-year stain and fade limited warranty. That combination of durability, safety, easy installation, and warranty is what makes it a leading PVC porch flooring choice.
