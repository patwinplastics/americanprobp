Questions

PVC Mouldings,
answered.

Common questions about American Pro cellular PVC trim, custom profiles, and samples.

What architectural mouldings does American Pro make?

American Pro manufactures 34 cellular PVC profiles in its full-line catalog: crowns and bed moulding; casings, brickmould and backband; sills and extenders; base and cap mouldings; exterior trim; coves, quarter round and panel mouldings; and beadboard and balusters. Custom profiles can also be tooled in-house at Patwin Plastics.

Will PVC mouldings rot or split outdoors?

No. Cellular PVC trim does not rot, split, or absorb moisture, which makes it the right choice for exterior trim around doors, windows, soffits, and water tables.

Can American Pro tool a custom profile?

Yes. Patwin Plastics has a full in-house tool room with wire EDM, sinker EDM, and polymer flow simulation. Custom profiles can be designed, tooled, and extruded under one roof in Linden, NJ.

How do I see what a moulding profile actually looks like?

Free hand samples of any standard profile can be requested through the contact form. We mail short cut sections so the actual profile and finish can be evaluated in person.

Which profiles are included in the full-line catalog?

The full-line catalog includes 34 profiles across seven product families, including Adam's Casing (WM97PVC), Farmhouse Sill (SILLFARMPVC), Water Table (WATERTABLEPVC), and 6-inch E&CB Beadboard (ECB6PVC). Additional profiles from the website collection are listed separately.

Are American Pro mouldings made in the USA?

Yes. Every profile is extruded by Patwin Plastics in Linden, New Jersey, including any custom profiles tooled in-house.
