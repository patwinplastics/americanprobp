How to use the Profile Designer

Drawing

  • Click to add points. Hold Shift to lock to 15° angles.
  • Click the first point, or press C, to close the shape.
  • Enter or double-click stops drawing.

Exact Dimensions

  • Type lengths like 1-5/8, 0.625 or 41.3 mm.
  • Angles: 0° right, 90° up, 180° left, -90° down.
  • Select a point to type its exact X and Y.

Rounds & Coves

  • Select a segment, then choose Round Out or Cove In.
  • Type the radius. Flip Curve reverses the direction.
  • Arc radii show in red on the drawing.

Moving Around

  • Scroll or pinch to zoom. Hold Space and drag to pan.
  • F fits the drawing, Ctrl Z undoes.
  • A sketch is fine. Our engineers confirm the final die geometry with you.
