Questions

Custom Profiles, Answered.

Can you match a historic or discontinued moulding?

Yes. Send a cut section or clear photos with a ruler in frame. We model the profile, 3D-print a prototype for you to check against the original, then cut the production die in our own tool room.

Can you build a profile an architect has specified?

Yes. Upload the section drawing or spec sheet and we work from it directly. You review a 3D-printed prototype for fit and feasibility before tooling is committed.

Do I need a CAD drawing?

No. You can sketch the profile in our free Profile Designer, or send a physical sample or good photos with a ruler in frame.

Can custom PVC mouldings be painted?

Yes. Cellular PVC mouldings cut, route and paint like premium millwork, and they won't rot, split or absorb moisture.

How are pricing, minimums and lead time set?

They depend on the profile's size and complexity and the quantity you need. Send your drawing or sample and we'll come back with a quote for your project.

Where are custom profiles made?

Every custom profile is designed, tooled and extruded by Patwin Plastics in Linden, New Jersey.
